Net Earnings Analysis

Analyze income minus expenses to understand actual earnings.

Purpose

Calculate the delivery driver’s real earnings after business expenses.

Simple Example

If income is $1,500 and expenses are $300, net earnings are $1,200.

Simple Bookkeeping Decision Rule

Net earnings should always be calculated as income minus business expenses. Gross income alone does not show the real financial result.

Screenshot: Net Earnings Analysis

Analysis

The project shows $13,006.80 in income and $2,863.77 in expenses, resulting in $10,143.03 in net earnings. This gives a clearer picture than income alone.

Result

The net earnings analysis shows the actual business result after expenses are deducted.
