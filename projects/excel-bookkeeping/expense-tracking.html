Business Expense Tracking

Track delivery-related expenses such as gas, car maintenance, phone, tolls, parking, and supplies.

Purpose

Record business expenses so net earnings and monthly spending can be calculated correctly.

Simple Example

A $78.40 gas purchase is recorded as a delivery business expense with a receipt note.

Simple Bookkeeping Decision Rule

Only business-related expenses should be entered in the tracker. Each expense should have a date, category, description, amount, and receipt note.

Screenshot: Business Expense Tracking

Analysis

The expense tracker includes 37 expense records totaling $2,863.77. Each expense includes a receipt status and a supporting document source such as a fuel receipt photo, phone bill PDF, repair invoice, insurance policy, parking receipt, or E-ZPass statement.

Result

Expenses are clearly documented and ready to support monthly bookkeeping review and possible tax deduction review.
