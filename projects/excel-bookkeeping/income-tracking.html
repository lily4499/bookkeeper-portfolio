Income Tracking by Platform

Track delivery income from each platform across the 3-month period.

Purpose

Record all income received from delivery platforms so weekly and monthly earnings can be reviewed accurately.

Simple Example

A Walmart Spark payout of $245.50 is recorded with the date, platform name, income type, amount, and notes.

Simple Bookkeeping Decision Rule

Every income record should include date, platform, income type, amount, and notes. Do not combine multiple platforms in one row because each platform must be easy to review separately.

Screenshot: Income Tracking by Platform

Analysis

The income tracker includes 25 income entries across Walmart Spark, Uber, DoorDash, and Lyft. Total simulated income is $13,006.80. This helps compare which platform generated the most revenue.

Result

Income is organized by platform and date, making it easier to calculate monthly totals and review earnings trends.
