Weekly Summary

Summarize income, expenses, net earnings, and miles by week.

Purpose

Review weekly performance and identify changes in income, spending, net earnings, and delivery mileage.

Simple Example

The week of 03/22/2025 - 03/31/2025 had the highest net earnings because income was strong and expenses were controlled.

Simple Bookkeeping Decision Rule

Weekly totals should be calculated from the income, expense, and mileage logs. The weekly summary should not be typed manually when source data is available.

Screenshot: Weekly Summary

Analysis

The weekly summary presents income, expenses, net earnings, and miles clearly for each week. Total weekly income ranges from $415.75 to $1,175.75. The highest net week is Jan 15 - Jan 21 with $955.11 net earnings. The highest mileage week is Feb 26 - Mar 04 with 710 miles.

Result

The weekly summary provides a clear review of weekly delivery performance, including income, expenses, net earnings, and mileage trends.
