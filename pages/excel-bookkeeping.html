Excel Bookkeeping Portfolio Project

Delivery Driver Income & Expense Tracker

A simulated 3-month bookkeeping project using Microsoft Excel for Uber, Walmart Spark, Lyft, and DoorDash delivery activity. Each month includes at least $4,000.00 total income, and Lyft includes both income and mileage records. This project supports target roles such as Junior Bookkeeper, Accounting Clerk, Accounts Payable Clerk, and Accounts Receivable Clerk.
